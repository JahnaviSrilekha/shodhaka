# Acme Bank ICT Incident and Continuity Policy

Status: Approved · Owner: Head of IT Operations · Version 2.1 (synthetic example, fictional company)

## Purpose

This policy describes how Acme Bank detects, handles and recovers from ICT incidents affecting
payment, card and online banking services.

## Governance

The Head of IT Operations owns this policy and approves changes. The policy is reviewed when the
IT department considers it necessary.

## Incident classification

Every ICT incident is logged in the service desk tool and given a priority from P1 to P4 based on
the number of affected customers and the duration of the outage. P1 incidents are escalated to the
on-call manager within 15 minutes.

## Reporting

P1 incidents are summarised in the monthly IT operations report to the CIO.

## Business continuity

Critical services have a disaster recovery site. The recovery time objective for card payments is
2 hours and the recovery point objective is 15 minutes. The recovery plan was last exercised in 2023.

## Backups

Production databases are backed up every night to a separate data centre. Backups are kept for
35 days.

## Third-party providers

Card processing and cloud hosting are outsourced to external ICT providers. Contracts are managed by
the procurement team.
