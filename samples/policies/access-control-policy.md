# Acme Bank Access Control Policy

Status: Approved by the Management Board · Version 1.4 (synthetic example, fictional company)

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and systems that process customer or company data.

## Principles

Access is granted on a need-to-know and least-privilege basis. Every account belongs to one named
person; shared accounts are not allowed.

## Granting access

Line managers request access through the identity portal. The system owner approves the request.

## Reviewing access

Access rights to critical systems are reviewed every quarter by the system owner. Rights that are no
longer needed are removed within five working days.

## Leavers

Accounts of leavers are disabled on their last working day by HR-triggered automation.

## Logging

Successful and failed logins to critical systems are logged centrally. Logs are kept for one year.
